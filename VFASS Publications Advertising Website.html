Booking conditions

  1. All prices are in Nigerian Naira.
  2. Prices apply per publication issue.
  3. Full payment confirms an advertising reservation.
  4. Advert placements are subject to availability.
  5. Advert artwork must be submitted before the production deadline.
  6. Design assistance attracts the stated additional fee.
  7. All advertisements are subject to publication approval.
  8. Sponsored content must be clearly identified.
  9. The production team reserves the right to reject inappropriate or misleading advertisements.
  10. Final advert dimensions and artwork specifications will be communicated during booking.
